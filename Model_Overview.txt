# Customer Purchase Prediction using K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)

## Project Overview

This project predicts whether a customer is likely to purchase a product based on customer information such as Gender, Age, and Salary using the K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) classification algorithm.

## Problem Statement

The objective is to help businesses identify potential customers who are likely to purchase a product, enabling more effective marketing strategies.

## Dataset

Features:
- Gender
- Age
- Salary

Target:
- Purchased (Yes/No)
